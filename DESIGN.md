# Assassin's Creed: Hindustan — Design Doc

Date: 2026-09-26
Status: Approved concept, MVP scoped
Author: brainstorm with user

## 1. Premise + MVP Scope

**Title:** Assassin's Creed: Hindustan (working)
**Logline:** 1656 Agra. Son of a murdered Mughal governor hunts his father's killers through court, fort and construction site — and finds a hidden Order pulling Dara Shikoh vs Aurangzeb toward war.

**Full vision (Succession Thriller):** 3 hubs — Agra → Shahjahanabad → Rajput fort-city + Deccan outpost. Spine = succession war 1657-58. Play both sides. Artifact: Koh-i-Noor as Piece of Eden fragment.

**MVP (M4 Air-safe):** Agra only, ~1.5km² dense map:
- Agra Fort (social stealth)
- Taj construction site 1656 (parkour vertical slice)
- Kinari Bazaar / Yamuna ghats (crowd stealth)
- Bureau haveli (safehouse)

**Pillars:**
1. Court stealth over brute combat
2. Investigation → assassination loop
3. Order vs freedom, never Hindu vs Muslim — all faiths on all sides

## 2. Protagonist + Templar Network

**Protagonist:** Arman, early 20s, son of Agra subahdar. Persian/Hindavi, court etiquette + shamsher training, not yet a killer. Power: status lets him walk into durbar, overseer's tent, merchant haveli. Social stealth first, blades second. Loves stealth fantasy.

**Hidden Ones (Agra cell):**
- Aging harkara (messenger-spy) — parkour / tailing mentor
- Sufi record-keeper — lore / Eden knowledge, runs bureau haveli
- Slow recruit: he earns entry by solving father's murder

**Templars (Order of the Iron Hand — working name):** Small secret network inside nobility, bureaucracy, military, merchant elite. Do NOT cause succession war; exploit Dara vs Aurangzeb to crown a centralized ruler they can steer. Different faiths, united by order ideology. Foreign letters hint at wider Order (Portuguese / Safavid contacts) but MVP keeps villains local — no "foreigners control India."

MVP shows 3 of 8:
1. Mir Munshi (paperwork — controls audience)
2. Kotwal's deputy (watch — controls guards)
3. Marble contractor (money + Taj access — funds both princes)

Father found ledger, was murdered staged as Rajput revenge.

**Gear (MVP):**
- Khanjar-bracer blade
- Smoke from fireworks-maker
- Coins / whistle lure, forged farman (1-time entry)
- No combat tree, no guard disguise in MVP

## 3. Story Spine (MVP: Agra 1656)

**Act 1 — Murder:** Father stabbed after durbar. Chase killer through bazaar crowds, fail. Bureau saves you. Clue: contractor steel.

**Act 2 — Investigation (any order):**
- Fort: eavesdrop Mir Munshi, steal farmans, durbar social-stealth
- City: tail deputy's patrol, bazaar rooftops, free informant
- Taj site: scaffold climb, crane / elephant stealth, find ledger paying both Dara + Aurangzeb agents
- Twist: father found it first

**Act 3 — Reveal:** Contractor flees to Yamuna fortlet. Ghat infiltration → assassinate. Dying words: "Eight of us. The war will crown our order." Dara vs Aurangzeb drums — hook for Delhi / Deccan full game.

MVP ends closed (revenge) + open (5 Templars left, war coming).

## 4. Stealth-First Gameplay (M4-safe)

**Loop:** Clue → Infiltrate → Assassinate / Spare → Vanish. No XP tree MVP, 4 tools earned via story.

1. Social stealth: crowd / worker blend, rupee bribe, forged farman. Disguises: noble (fort), mazdoor (Taj). No guard disguise MVP.
2. Parkour: fort walls (1 breach), Taj scaffolds, bazaar rooftops. One-button climb + air assassinate. No trees / cliffs MVP.
3. Detection: unseen / suspicious / hunted. Hide: zenana screens, hay carts, Yamuna reeds, tents. Whistle + coin.
4. Kills: hidden blade, haystack / ledge / corner kills, smoke escape. Open combat 2-3 guards max — you should lose, pushes stealth.
5. Investigation: eavesdrop circles, tailing, pickpocket ledger keys. "Hush" pulse (Eagle vision lite) marks enemies / clues only.

**Perf rules:** <60 active AI, crowd groups 4-5, fog hides far city, no ray-tracing, no horses / naval / notoriety / recruits in MVP.

## 5. Agra MVP Map

1. Agra Fort — 2 courts + zenana + durbar. Social stealth, 15 guards max.
2. Taj construction — half-dome + scaffolds + ramps. Parkour playground.
3. Kinari Bazaar — rooftops, crowd blend, tails.
4. Yamuna ghats + reeds — water-edge infiltration / escape.
5. Bureau haveli — contracts, informants, no combat.

**Skipped for MVP (add when Agra loop is fun):** Delhi, Rajput fort, Deccan, horses, naval, notoriety, Brotherhood recruits, full Eden powers.

## Success Criteria (MVP)

- 20-min vertical slice: 1 investigation → 1 fort infiltration → 1 assassination → escape runs 30fps on M4 Air
- Stealth feels AC: social + parkour + hiding all usable
- Story hook lands without religious framing
